## 1. Line Listing
<!-- page: 1 -->

This line listing documents all Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (SUSARs) identified across the revarafusp alfa program during the second quarter of 2026, prepared in accordance with the sponsor's expedited safety reporting procedure. Three SUSARs were reported in Q2 2026 and all were expedited to the FDA within 15 calendar days. This timeline is measured from the sponsor becoming aware of each event, consistent with the 15-calendar-day expedited reporting requirement for serious, unexpected, and at least reasonably possibly related adverse events under 21 CFR 312.32. Participant 003-0007, date of birth 14 March 1958, experienced a Grade 4 pneumonitis SUSAR. The event occurred on study day 96 of AXIOM-HN01, presenting with progressive dyspnea and bilateral ground-glass opacities on chest computed tomography; it was treated with high-dose corticosteroids and study treatment was permanently discontinued. The remaining two SUSARs, detailed in the full line listing appendix, involved Grade 4 infusion-related reaction and Grade 3 hepatic transaminase elevation with associated jaundice, respectively, both of which have since resolved.
