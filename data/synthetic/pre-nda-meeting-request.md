## 1. Meeting Request
<!-- page: 1 -->

This package is submitted to request a milestone meeting in advance of a planned New Drug Application submission for revarafusp alfa in combination with torilizumab. Meridian requests a Type B pre-NDA meeting with the FDA Division of Oncology 2. The meeting will discuss the adequacy of the planned NDA content, including the clinical, nonclinical, and chemistry, manufacturing, and controls sections, ahead of a planned submission following the AXIOM-HN01 primary analysis. The pre-NDA meeting is scheduled for 12 November 2026. This date follows the Division's confirmation of meeting date availability within the standard 75-day scheduling window for Type B meetings. The meeting package is due to the FDA 30 days in advance, on 13 October 2026. The package will include the questions listed in Section 2 along with supporting background materials summarizing the AXIOM-HN01 study design, interim safety experience, and proposed regulatory strategy.

## 2. Questions for the Agency
<!-- page: 4 -->

This section lists the questions Meridian proposes to discuss with the Division at the pre-NDA meeting. Question 1 asks whether the proposed OS analysis population is acceptable for registration. Specifically, the question is whether the PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 population, consistent with the protocol-specified primary endpoint, is an acceptable basis for the primary efficacy claim, or whether the Division would prefer analyses in the intent-to-treat population as well. Question 2 asks whether the proposed pediatric study plan components are adequate given the rarity of this indication in the pediatric population. Question 3 asks whether the immunogenicity assay validation package is sufficient. This question addresses whether the planned characterization of anti-drug antibody incidence and its impact on pharmacokinetics and safety is adequate, given that revarafusp alfa is a novel bifunctional biologic without a direct precedent assay. Additional questions concerning the chemistry, manufacturing, and controls section are provided in Appendix A.
