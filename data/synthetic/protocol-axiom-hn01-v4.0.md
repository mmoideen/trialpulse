## 1. Study Design
<!-- page: 1 -->

AXIOM-HN01 is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study. This protocol amendment (version 4.0) updates the study design based on emerging enrollment and safety experience while preserving the fundamental double-blind, placebo-controlled structure established in version 3.0. Planned enrollment is increased to 528 participants across 104 sites. The additional participants and sites were added to preserve statistical power for overall survival following a blinded review of the assumed control-arm event rate. Participants are randomized 1:1 to revarafusp alfa plus torilizumab or placebo plus torilizumab. Randomization continues to be stratified by PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) category, HPV status of the oropharynx, and ECOG performance status at baseline, and remains centrally managed through the interactive response technology (IRT) system. All sites active under version 3.0 continue participation under version 4.0 following local IRB or ethics committee approval, and newly activated sites are initiated directly under version 4.0.

## 2. Primary Endpoint
<!-- page: 3 -->

The primary endpoint is overall survival (OS) in the PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 population. This endpoint is unchanged from protocol version 3.0. Progression-free survival was elevated from an exploratory endpoint to a key secondary endpoint in version 4.0. This change reflects feedback from the FDA Division of Oncology 2 during the End of Phase 2 meeting, which recommended progression-free survival be collected with the same rigor as the primary endpoint, given its relevance to regulatory decision-making in this indication. The study is powered at 90% to detect a hazard ratio of 0.70. The increased enrollment described in Section 1 preserves this power under the revised control-arm event-rate assumptions incorporated into version 4.0. No change was made to the alpha-spending approach or to the definition of the PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 analysis population.

## 3. Key Inclusion Criteria
<!-- page: 5 -->

The inclusion criteria for AXIOM-HN01 version 4.0 largely carry forward from version 3.0, with one substantive addition described below. Participants must be at least 18 years of age. Participants must have histologically or cytologically confirmed squamous cell carcinoma of the oropharynx, oral cavity, hypopharynx, or larynx that is recurrent or metastatic. ECOG performance status must be 0 or 1. This is assessed at the time of screening, consistent with protocol version 3.0. Participants must have measurable disease per RECIST v1.1. Version 4.0 permits enrollment of participants with treated, stable brain metastases. Eligibility requires that treatment was completed at least four weeks prior to randomization, that the participant is neurologically stable without a corticosteroid requirement beyond a physiologic replacement dose, and that follow-up brain imaging confirms no evidence of progression. This addition reflects emerging real-world experience suggesting that clinically stable, treated brain metastases did not meaningfully affect systemic treatment tolerability in torilizumab-based combinations, and that their prior exclusion was disproportionately limiting enrollment given how frequently brain metastases occur in this population.

## 4. Dose and Administration
<!-- page: 9 -->

Dosing procedures for AXIOM-HN01 version 4.0 are unchanged except for the new observation requirement described below. Revarafusp alfa is administered at 1500 mg intravenously every 3 weeks. Torilizumab is administered at 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks. Version 4.0 adds a mandatory 60-minute observation period after the first infusion. This requirement was added following review of infusion-related reaction reports summarized in the Development Safety Update Report, which showed that the majority of Grade 3 or higher infusion-related reactions occurred within the first hour after the initial dose. Sites must have emergency equipment and trained personnel available throughout the observation period. Subsequent infusions may follow the standard post-infusion monitoring procedure described in the Investigator Brochure unless a participant experienced an infusion-related reaction at a prior cycle, in which case the extended observation period is repeated at the next scheduled infusion.

## 5. Interim Analysis
<!-- page: 14 -->

The statistical analysis plan was updated in protocol version 4.0 to add an earlier look at the data. Version 4.0 adds a second interim analysis after 40% of target OS events. This earlier interim analysis uses a more conservative O'Brien-Fleming boundary than the analysis planned after 60% of events, reflecting the smaller information fraction available at that time. The interim analysis is reviewed by an independent Data Monitoring Committee. The Committee's charter, membership, and unblinding procedures are unchanged from version 3.0; only the number and timing of scheduled efficacy looks has changed. As with the original interim analysis, the Committee retains authority to recommend early stopping for efficacy, futility, or safety at either scheduled look, and its recommendations are communicated to the sponsor's unblinded statistician rather than to the blinded study team.

## 6. Summary of Changes from Version 3.0
<!-- page: 21 -->

This section summarizes the substantive changes incorporated into protocol version 4.0 relative to version 3.0, consistent with the summary of changes submitted to the FDA under IND Amendment 12. Enrollment increased from 480 to 528 participants. Site count increased from 92 to 104. These two changes were made together to preserve statistical power for the overall survival primary endpoint following a blinded reassessment of the assumed control-arm event rate. Brain metastases exclusion criterion was relaxed. Participants with treated, stable brain metastases meeting the criteria in Section 3 may now enroll. A second interim analysis was added. It occurs at 40% of target OS events, in addition to the previously planned analysis at 60% of events. A 60-minute post-infusion observation period was added. It follows the first dose of study treatment, based on the timing pattern of infusion-related reactions observed to date. Site personnel should review the full protocol text for the complete rationale supporting each change; this section is intended as a navigational summary only.
