## 1. Purpose of Amendment
<!-- page: 1 -->

This IND amendment is submitted pursuant to 21 CFR 312.30 to notify the FDA of changes to the AXIOM-HN01 protocol. Amendment 12 submits AXIOM-HN01 Protocol version 4.0 to the FDA. The submission includes the associated summary of changes, an updated informed consent form template, and Investigator Brochure Edition 6, which contains the reference safety information supporting the protocol changes. Amendment 12 was submitted on 09 June 2026 and the 30-day review period ended on 09 July 2026. The review period elapsed without a clinical hold or request for additional information from the Division, permitting sites to implement protocol version 4.0 following local IRB or ethics committee approval. The sponsor notified all active sites of FDA acknowledgment immediately following expiration of the 30-day review period.

## 2. Pending Amendments
<!-- page: 2 -->

Two additional amendments to this IND are in preparation and are summarized here for the Division's awareness ahead of formal submission. Amendment 13 covering the updated statistical analysis plan is pending and targeted for submission in September 2026. This amendment will formalize the two-interim-analysis design described in Protocol version 4.0, Section 5, including the revised alpha-spending boundaries for both the 40% and 60% information fraction looks. Amendment 14 covering the AXIOM-HN02 dose expansion cohort is pending internal review. This amendment, once finalized, will add a dose expansion cohort to AXIOM-HN02 to further characterize the safety and preliminary efficacy of revarafusp alfa in combination with chemoradiotherapy prior to a potential randomized Phase 3 study in the locally advanced setting. Neither amendment is expected to require a change to the currently enrolling protocol versions.
