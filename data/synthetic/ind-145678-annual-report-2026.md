## 1. Summary of Clinical Progress
<!-- page: 1 -->

This annual report is submitted pursuant to 21 CFR 312.33 to summarize the clinical progress of the revarafusp alfa development program under IND 145678 during the reporting period. As of 31 March 2026, impacted studies under IND 145678 have enrolled 412 participants cumulatively. This reflects continued site activation and enrollment momentum since the prior annual report. Two studies were active during the reporting period: AXIOM-HN01 and AXIOM-HN02. AXIOM-HN01 continued enrollment under protocol version 3.0 for the majority of the reporting period, transitioning to version 4.0 following FDA acknowledgment of Amendment 12 in July 2026. AXIOM-HN02 activated its first sites during the reporting period and began enrolling participants in the first-line locally advanced setting. No new studies were initiated under this IND during the reporting period, and no studies were terminated or placed on clinical hold.

## 3. Changes in the Investigator Brochure
<!-- page: 6 -->

The Investigator Brochure was updated once during the reporting period to incorporate additional safety experience accrued across the program. Investigator Brochure Edition 6 was distributed to all active sites in May 2026. This followed sponsor approval of the updated reference safety information, including the addition of hypertension as an identified risk and the revised incidence estimate for Grade 3 or higher acneiform dermatitis. Sites were required to acknowledge receipt of Edition 6 and to update site-specific informed consent documents where the revised reference safety information affected the description of expected risks. No sites reported delays in Edition 6 distribution or acknowledgment beyond the standard 30-day implementation window specified in the sponsor's document control procedure.
