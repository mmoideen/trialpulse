## 3. Cumulative Exposure
<!-- page: 4 -->

This Development Safety Update Report (DSUR) covers the reporting period ending 31 March 2026 and summarizes cumulative safety experience across all studies conducted under IND 145678. Cumulative exposure across the revarafusp alfa program is 1,284 participant-years as of 31 March 2026. This figure is calculated from the sum of individual participant treatment durations across AXIOM-HN01, AXIOM-HN02, and the completed Phase 1 studies that preceded them. It provides the denominator against which adverse event incidence rates presented elsewhere in this report should be interpreted, and represents a substantial increase over the exposure reported in the prior year's DSUR, reflecting the ramp-up of enrollment in AXIOM-HN01 following the increase in planned enrollment under protocol version 4.0.

## 6. Signal Evaluation
<!-- page: 11 -->

This section documents the formal signal evaluation activities conducted during the DSUR reporting period, consistent with the sponsor's safety signal management procedure. Hypertension was evaluated as an emerging safety signal and added to the reference safety information. This followed a disproportionality analysis and clinical review that identified a numerically elevated rate of Grade 3 or higher hypertension relative to background rates and relative to the torilizumab-alone comparator arm in AXIOM-HN01. The evaluation concluded that a causal association with revarafusp alfa, mediated by TGF-beta pathway inhibition, was plausible, resulting in the Investigator Brochure Edition 6 update described elsewhere in this report. No new signal was identified for hepatotoxicity during the reporting period. This is despite continued proactive monitoring given the class-wide association between EGFR-targeted therapies and hepatic transaminase elevations; liver function test abnormalities observed to date have been low-grade, transient, and have not met the sponsor's pre-specified signal evaluation threshold.
