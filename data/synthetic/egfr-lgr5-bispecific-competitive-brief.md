## 1. Program Overview
<!-- page: 1 -->

This brief summarizes publicly available information on a competing bispecific antibody program targeting EGFR and LGR5 in first-line recurrent or metastatic HNSCC, one of the four late-stage bispecific competitors identified in the current competitive landscape update. The competing EGFR/LGR5 bispecific reported a 63 percent objective response rate in a Phase 2 first-line cohort. This result was presented at a major oncology conference and is a notably higher response rate than has historically been observed with torilizumab monotherapy or chemotherapy-based regimens in this setting, though cross-trial comparisons should be interpreted cautiously given differences in patient selection and response assessment methodology. The competing program initiated its Phase 3 study in March 2026. This is approximately eight months ahead of anticipated timelines based on the sponsor's prior public guidance, suggesting the sponsor is prioritizing rapid registrational development following the strong Phase 2 signal. Meridian's competitive intelligence function will continue to monitor public disclosures from this program for updates on enrollment pace and study design.
