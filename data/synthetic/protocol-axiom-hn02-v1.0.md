## 1. Study Design
<!-- page: 1 -->

AXIOM-HN02 is an open-label Phase 2 study in first-line locally advanced HNSCC. The study is designed to evaluate revarafusp alfa in combination with concurrent chemoradiotherapy in participants who have not previously received systemic or radiation therapy for their disease. Planned enrollment is 120 participants across 28 sites. Sites are located in the United States and Western Europe and were selected based on experience delivering concurrent chemoradiotherapy protocols and managing the dermatologic toxicities associated with EGFR-targeted agents. Unlike AXIOM-HN01, AXIOM-HN02 does not include a placebo control; all participants receive revarafusp alfa alongside standard-of-care chemoradiotherapy, and the study is designed to characterize the safety and preliminary efficacy of this combination ahead of a possible randomized Phase 3 study in the locally advanced setting. Enrollment is expected to complete within approximately 18 months of the first site activation.

## 2. Primary Endpoint
<!-- page: 3 -->

The primary endpoint of AXIOM-HN02 is objective response rate per RECIST v1.1. Response is assessed by the investigator at the completion of concurrent chemoradiotherapy. Objective response rate was selected as the primary endpoint for this Phase 2 study because it is measurable within months of treatment completion, allowing an efficient early read on the activity of the combination before committing to a larger and longer Phase 3 program. Secondary endpoints include duration of response, progression-free survival, locoregional control rate, and the incidence and severity of treatment-related adverse events, particularly dermatologic and mucosal toxicities that may be additive between revarafusp alfa and radiotherapy. An exploratory endpoint evaluates circulating tumor DNA clearance as an early biomarker of response, which may inform patient selection strategies for a subsequent randomized study.

## 3. Dose and Administration
<!-- page: 7 -->

Dosing of revarafusp alfa in AXIOM-HN02 differs from the AXIOM-HN01 regimen to align with the concurrent chemoradiotherapy schedule. Revarafusp alfa is administered at 1500 mg intravenously every 2 weeks in AXIOM-HN02. Dosing continues at this schedule for the duration of concurrent chemoradiotherapy, followed by continuation at the same dose and schedule during a consolidation phase of up to six additional cycles in participants without disease progression. The every-2-week schedule was selected to maintain adequate drug exposure across the compressed radiotherapy treatment window, which is typically six to seven weeks in duration. Standard premedication and infusion monitoring procedures from the Investigator Brochure apply. Radiation therapy is delivered per institutional standard using either intensity-modulated radiation therapy or volumetric modulated arc therapy, with concurrent cisplatin-based chemotherapy administered per investigator discretion consistent with local standard of care.
