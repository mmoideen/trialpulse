## 1. Protocol Version 4.0 Rollout
<!-- page: 1 -->

Dear Investigators, This newsletter provides a program-wide update on the rollout of AXIOM-HN01 protocol version 4.0 and on overall enrollment progress. Protocol version 4.0 became effective on 02 June 2026 and all sites must implement it after IRB approval. Implementation is not permitted prior to local IRB or ethics committee approval, even at sites that have already received the amended documents from the sponsor. Sites should submit Protocol version 4.0 to their IRB within 14 days of receipt. Prompt submission minimizes the gap between sites operating under different protocol versions and supports timely re-consent of currently enrolled participants where required. A summary of the key changes in version 4.0, including the increased enrollment target, the relaxed brain metastases exclusion criterion, and the new post-infusion observation requirement, is available in the protocol's Section 6 and was also distributed separately to site principal investigators.

## 2. Enrollment Update
<!-- page: 2 -->

We also want to share an enrollment update across the AXIOM-HN01 program. Cumulative AXIOM-HN01 enrollment reached 366 participants as of 31 May 2026. This represents steady progress toward the revised target of 528 participants under protocol version 4.0, and the sponsor anticipates that the increased site count activated over the coming months will support continued enrollment momentum. We thank all participating sites for their continued dedication to timely enrollment and high-quality data collection, and encourage sites that have not yet reached their individual enrollment targets to reach out to their clinical trial manager for support with local recruitment strategies. As always, please do not hesitate to contact the study team with any questions about protocol version 4.0 or the enrollment update above.
