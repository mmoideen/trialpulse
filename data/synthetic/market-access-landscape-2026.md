## 2. Payer Considerations

This is consistent with existing coverage policy for torilizumab in this indication, meaning any future revarafusp alfa combination regimen would likely inherit similar biomarker-testing coverage requirements rather than facing a novel payer hurdle. This estimate is based on wholesale acquisition cost benchmarking against currently marketed combination regimens in this indication and typical treatment duration observed in real-world claims data. Payers are expected to apply standard prior authorization and step-therapy-adjacent utilization management consistent with existing oncology combination regimens, and no unusual access barriers specific to a bispecific mechanism of action are anticipated based on current payer policy trends.
