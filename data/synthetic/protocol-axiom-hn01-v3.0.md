## 1. Study Design
<!-- page: 1 -->

AXIOM-HN01 is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study. The study evaluates revarafusp alfa in combination with torilizumab against torilizumab alone in participants with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (R/M HNSCC) who have not received prior systemic therapy in the recurrent or metastatic setting. Planned enrollment is 480 participants across 92 sites. These sites span North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region, reflecting the site capability required to support central randomization, cold-chain drug handling, and blinded safety reporting at scale. Participants are randomized 1:1 to revarafusp alfa plus torilizumab or placebo plus torilizumab. Randomization is stratified by PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) category, human papillomavirus (HPV) status of the oropharynx, and Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status at baseline, and is performed centrally through an interactive response technology (IRT) system to preserve allocation concealment. Study drug is dispensed by an unblinded pharmacist at each site who has no other study responsibilities, and all other study personnel, including the investigator and clinical staff assessing efficacy or safety, remain unaware of treatment assignment until database lock for the primary analysis.

## 2. Primary Endpoint
<!-- page: 3 -->

The primary endpoint is overall survival (OS) in the PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 population. This population represents the majority of participants enrolled in AXIOM-HN01 and is consistent with the population studied in prior torilizumab registrational trials in R/M HNSCC. Key secondary endpoints include overall survival in the intent-to-treat population, progression-free survival by blinded independent central review, objective response rate, duration of response, and patient-reported outcomes measured using the EORTC QLQ-C30 and QLQ-H&N35 instruments. The study is powered at 90% to detect a hazard ratio of 0.70. This assumes a two-sided alpha of 0.05 for the primary comparison and accounts for the one planned interim analysis described in Section 5. The sample size calculation assumes a median overall survival of approximately 12 months in the control arm, consistent with published outcomes for torilizumab monotherapy in this population, and an enrollment period of approximately 24 months followed by a minimum follow-up period sufficient to accrue the target number of OS events.

## 3. Key Inclusion Criteria
<!-- page: 5 -->

Enrollment in AXIOM-HN01 requires that participants meet all protocol-specified inclusion criteria prior to randomization. Participants must be at least 18 years of age. Participants (or their legally authorized representative) must also provide written informed consent prior to any study-specific procedures, including screening for PD-L1 and HPV status. Participants must have histologically or cytologically confirmed squamous cell carcinoma of the oropharynx, oral cavity, hypopharynx, or larynx that is recurrent or metastatic and not amenable to curative-intent local therapy. ECOG performance status must be 0 or 1. This reflects a functional status sufficient to tolerate combination immunotherapy and protocol-mandated study visits, including biweekly infusions. Participants must have measurable disease per RECIST v1.1. Assessment is made by the investigator, with at least one target lesion that has not been previously irradiated unless there is documented progression at that site. Adequate hematologic, hepatic, and renal function are also required, along with archival or fresh tumor tissue sufficient for central PD-L1 and HPV testing prior to randomization.

## 4. Dose and Administration
<!-- page: 9 -->

Study treatment is administered in 21-day cycles until documented disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, withdrawal of consent, or completion of the maximum permitted duration of therapy. Revarafusp alfa is administered at 1500 mg intravenously every 3 weeks. It is given as a fixed-dose infusion over approximately 60 minutes, with the infusion duration extendable per site standard operating procedure if an infusion-related reaction occurs. Torilizumab is administered at 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks. It is infused immediately following completion of the revarafusp alfa infusion and any associated observation period. Premedication with an antihistamine is recommended prior to the first two cycles, given the known association between revarafusp alfa and infusion-related reactions described in the Investigator Brochure. Dose modifications for revarafusp alfa follow the toxicity management guidance in the current Investigator Brochure edition in effect at each site; torilizumab dose modifications follow the current approved product label. No intra-participant dose escalation is permitted, and dose re-escalation after a dose reduction is not permitted under this protocol version.

## 5. Interim Analysis
<!-- page: 14 -->

The statistical analysis plan for AXIOM-HN01 pre-specifies one formal interim analysis of overall survival prior to the final analysis. A single interim analysis is planned after 60% of target OS events. This threshold is expected to be reached in the PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 population, at which point the O'Brien-Fleming alpha-spending boundary permits early stopping for overwhelming efficacy. The interim analysis is reviewed by an independent Data Monitoring Committee. The Committee is composed of oncologists, a biostatistician, and a patient advocate, none of whom are otherwise involved in the conduct of the study. It also conducts unblinded safety reviews on a quarterly cadence independent of the efficacy interim analysis, with authority to recommend study modification, temporary suspension of enrollment, or early termination if the safety profile is unfavorable. Recommendations from the Committee are transmitted to the sponsor's unblinded statistician and are not shared with the blinded study team unless a protocol amendment is required as a result.
