## 1. Attendees
<!-- page: 1 -->

These minutes document the End of Phase 2 meeting between Meridian Therapeutics and the FDA regarding the revarafusp alfa development program. The meeting was held on 08 October 2025 with the FDA Division of Oncology 2. Agency attendees included the clinical team lead, a statistical reviewer, a pharmacology/toxicology reviewer, and a regulatory project manager; Meridian attendees included clinical, regulatory, biostatistics, and nonclinical representatives, along with an external statistical consultant. The Meridian regulatory lead of record is Alina Ferreira. They presented the proposed Phase 3 study design and moderated the discussion of Agency questions. These minutes were prepared by Meridian following the meeting and were provided to the Division for concurrence within the standard 30-calendar-day timeframe; no substantive corrections were requested by the Division.

## 2. Agency Feedback
<!-- page: 3 -->

This section summarizes the Division's key feedback on the proposed Phase 3 study design for AXIOM-HN01. The Agency agreed that overall survival is an acceptable primary endpoint. This position is consistent with prior approvals of checkpoint-inhibitor-based regimens in recurrent or metastatic HNSCC. The Agency recommended that progression-free survival be collected as a key secondary endpoint. The Division noted that a well-controlled secondary analysis of progression-free survival could support labeling discussions even if not required for the primary efficacy claim. The Division did not object to the proposed PD-L1 CPS greater than or equal to 1 analysis population but requested that the intent-to-treat population also be analyzed and reported. This feedback was incorporated into AXIOM-HN01 protocol version 4.0, as summarized in that protocol's Section 6.
