## 1. Active Combination Studies
<!-- page: 1 -->

This tracker catalogs actively enrolling clinical studies combining torilizumab with an investigational agent in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, updated monthly from public trial registries. Twenty-two active torilizumab combination studies in HNSCC were identified in June 2026. These studies span a range of combination partners including bispecific antibodies, antibody-drug conjugates, oncolytic viruses, and small-molecule targeted agents, reflecting sustained interest in improving on torilizumab monotherapy outcomes in this indication. Nine of those studies are sponsored by academic consortia. This is a notably higher academic share than is typical in most solid tumor indications, likely reflecting strong investigator interest in exploring novel combination hypotheses ahead of industry-led registrational programs. The full tracker, including study phase, combination partner mechanism, and estimated primary completion date for each of the 22 studies, is available in the appendix to this report.
