## 1. Deviation Description
<!-- page: 1 -->

This notice documents a protocol deviation identified at site US-027 and reported to the sponsor in accordance with the site's standard operating procedures. Site US-027 reported a major protocol deviation on 27 May 2026 involving an out-of-window tumor assessment. The deviation affected a participant enrolled in AXIOM-HN01 and was discovered during the site's routine review of upcoming scheduled assessments. The tumor assessment was performed 11 days outside the protocol-specified window. The delay resulted from a scheduling conflict with the participant's availability for the required computed tomography scan, and it was not identified by site staff until after the assessment had already occurred. The deviation was classified as major because the assessment supports the primary and key secondary efficacy endpoints, and out-of-window imaging assessments have the potential to introduce bias into the timing of efficacy determinations. The sponsor's medical monitor reviewed the deviation and determined that the resulting imaging data remain evaluable and do not require the assessment to be excluded from the efficacy analysis.

## 2. Corrective and Preventive Action
<!-- page: 2 -->

To prevent recurrence of this deviation, the following corrective and preventive action is required. Site US-027 must retrain all study coordinators on visit windows by 30 June 2026. Training must cover the protocol-specified visit window tolerances for all efficacy assessments, the site's process for flagging upcoming assessments at risk of falling outside the permitted window, and escalation procedures when a scheduling conflict cannot be resolved within the allowed window. Documentation of training completion, including sign-in sheets or electronic training records for every study coordinator, must be filed in the site regulatory binder and made available to the monitor at the next scheduled visit. The sponsor's clinical trial manager will follow up with the site prior to the 30 June 2026 deadline to confirm training has been scheduled.
