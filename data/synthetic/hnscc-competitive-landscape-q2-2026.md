## 1. Late-Stage Competitors
<!-- page: 1 -->

This landscape update summarizes the late-stage competitive environment in recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (R/M HNSCC) as of the second quarter of 2026. Four bispecific programs are in Phase 3 development for first-line R/M HNSCC as of Q2 2026. This count includes revarafusp alfa and three competitor molecules targeting overlapping or adjacent mechanisms, reflecting broad industry conviction that bispecific approaches can outperform single-agent checkpoint inhibition in this indication. Torilizumab monotherapy remains the standard of care comparator in first-line PD-L1 positive disease. It is the active comparator or backbone combination partner in nearly every ongoing late-stage study in this setting, reinforcing its position as the benchmark against which new combination regimens must demonstrate meaningful improvement. Competitive intensity in this indication has increased notably over the past two years as multiple companies have advanced bispecific and combination strategies out of Phase 1/2 development into registrational trials.
